What types of brands do you work with? +
We work with fashion, beauty, lifestyle, F&B, tech and event brands — primarily those looking to build a strong social media presence and content strategy in the UAE and GCC market.
How does a project typically start? +
Every project begins with a discovery call where we learn about your brand, goals and audience. From there we build a custom strategy and creative direction before any production begins.
Do you offer monthly retainers? +
Yes — our most popular packages are monthly content retainers covering strategy, production, editing and reporting. We also do one-off project work for events and campaigns.
Where are you based and do you travel? +
We're based in Dubai, UAE and work across the entire GCC region. We're also open to international projects — just reach out and we'll figure it out together.
What's your turnaround time? +
For retainer clients, content is delivered on a rolling weekly or biweekly schedule. For campaigns and events, standard turnaround is 5–10 business days post-shoot.
How do I get started? +
Simple — book a free 30-minute discovery call via Calendly and we'll take it from there. No commitment needed for the first call.
